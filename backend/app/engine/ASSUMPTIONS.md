# Filling in `assumptions.json`

Every number the engine uses comes from [`assumptions.json`](assumptions.json). Right now each block says `"source": "PLACEHOLDER"`, which means the values are rough guesses. To fill a block: replace its numbers with sourced ones, in the units below, and replace `PLACEHOLDER` with the source. **Houston only**: there is one city block, `cities.Houston`.

Rules that apply to every field:
- **Rates and fractions are decimals.** 7% is `0.07`, not `7`.
- **Money is USD, energy is kWh per year, carbon is kg CO2.** If your source uses lb/MWh, $/W or monthly figures, convert, and say so in the source.
- **One `source` per block** covers every number in that block. If numbers in one block come from different places, list them all, separated by `;`.
- **A valid source** names the publisher, the document or tool, the exact table or setting, the geography, the year, and a URL, plus any conversion you did. For example: `EPA eGRID2023, subregion ERCT, CO2 total output emission rate 771 lb/MWh -> /2204.6 = 0.350 kg/kWh, https://www.epa.gov/egrid`. (The 771 here only shows the format. Use the real figure.)
- **Verify every incentive.** All six `incentive_fraction` and `incentive_p` values are guesses. For each upgrade type, confirm that a program actually exists for a Houston commercial building starting a project now, then put its rate, its deadline and the date you checked it in the source. Federal clean-energy incentives changed in 2025, so don't rely on anything published before then.

Paths use dots: `cities.Houston.grid_kg_co2_per_kwh` means `"cities" → "Houston" → "grid_kg_co2_per_kwh"`. `upgrades.<type>` rows apply to each of the six types: `rooftop_solar`, `cool_roof`, `window_upgrade`, `insulation`, `heat_pump`, `led_retrofit`.

**Scales with** says what the engine divides the upgrade's size by to get coverage (0–1), then multiplies savings by. Coverage 1 means the upgrade covers all of it. "Fixed" means savings are not scaled.

| `global.n_scenarios` | count | — | Number of random scenarios simulated per upgrade. A model setting, not a fact. | None needed (setting). Keep 10000. |
| `global.n_scenarios_optimizer` | count | — | Scenarios the optimizer uses when it accounts for risk. Smaller than `n_scenarios` to keep it under 1 s. | None needed (setting). Keep 1000. |
| `global.seed` | integer | — | Random seed. Same seed gives the same results every run. | None needed (setting). |
| `global.discount_rate` | decimal per year | — | Annual rate used to turn future savings into today's dollars. Covered by `global.source`. |
| `global.general_inflation` | decimal per year | — | General price inflation. Use the same nominal basis as `discount_rate`. | `Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia, Survey of Professional Forecasters, 10-year CPI inflation forecast, <quarter, year>, https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/real-time-data-research/survey-of-professional-forecasters`, or CBO's long-term CPI projection with the report date. | `global.energy_price_growth.sd` | decimal per year | — | Uncertainty in that growth rate (1 standard deviation). | `global.carbon_price.p_fee_starts` | probability 0–1 | — | Chance that a carbon fee applies to these buildings during the upgrade's life. | `global.carbon_price.start_year_range` | [min, max] years from today | — | If a fee starts, the year it starts is drawn uniformly from this range. | Same style as above: a stated team assumption with the reasoning. |
| `global.carbon_price.price_range_per_tonne` | [min, max] USD per tonne CO2 | — | If a fee starts, its price is drawn uniformly from this range. | Anchor to a published price, e.g. `California cap-and-trade auction settlement price, CARB, <quarter, year>, https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/...` for the low end and `EPA social cost of carbon (2023 report), <year, rate>` for the high end. |

| `building_estimates.source` | text | — | Source for the floor height. | See row above. |
| `cities.Houston.electricity_rate_usd_per_kwh` | USD per kWh | — | Average price commercial customers in Houston pay for electricity, all charges included. | `EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A, Texas, commercial sector, 2025 annual average, cents/kWh / 100, https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/` |
| `cities.Houston.grid_kg_co2_per_kwh` | kg CO2 per kWh | — | CO2 emitted per kWh drawn from Houston's grid (ERCOT). The engine counts each kWh saved or generated as this much CO2 avoided. | `EPA eGRID2023, subregion ERCT, CO2 total output emission rate (lb/MWh) / 2204.6, https://www.epa.gov/egrid` |
| `cities.Houston.solar_kwh_per_kw_year` | kWh per kW per year | — | Electricity one kW of rooftop solar produces per year in Houston, after normal system losses. | `NREL PVWatts v8, Houston TX, 1 kW DC, fixed open rack, tilt 20°, azimuth 180°, losses 14%, annual AC output, https://pvwatts.nrel.gov/` |
| `cities.Houston.kwh_per_sqft_year.<type>` | kWh per sqft per year | — | Typical annual electricity use per square foot for each benchmark type (`Office`, `Retail`, `Industrial`, `Multi-Family`, `Other`). Used as the baseline when there are no utility bills. `Other` covers any CoStar type not mapped in `buildings.py`, so use an all-buildings average. | `EIA CBECS 2018, Table C20 (electricity intensity by building activity), West South Central or South region, kWh/sqft, https://www.eia.gov/consumption/commercial/`. Use RECS or Energy Star Portfolio Manager for Multi-Family. |
| `cities.Houston.source` | text | — | Sources for the four city fields above. | List each, separated by `;`. |
| `upgrades.<type>.cost_unit` | text | — | Unit `unit_cost` is in. Must match the unit the upgrade is sized in: `usd_per_kw` for `rooftop_solar`, `usd_per_sqft` for the other five. Don't change it. | None needed (fixed). |
| `upgrades.<type>.unit_cost` | USD per kW or USD per sqft (see `cost_unit`) | — | Installed cost per unit, before any incentive: equipment, labor, permits. Units: solar per kW (DC); cool roof per sqft of roof; windows per sqft of glass; insulation per sqft of wall; heat pump and LED per sqft of floor served. | `NREL Annual Technology Baseline 2024, commercial PV, moderate case, 2026 capex $/kW, https://atb.nrel.gov/`, or a dated contractor quote or cost guide (e.g. RSMeans <year>) with the line item. |
| `upgrades.rooftop_solar.savings_mu` | decimal 0–1 | Modeled generation (kW × `solar_kwh_per_kw_year`) | Average fraction of modeled generation the system actually delivers. Around 0.95 means 5% underperformance. Not a share of the building's spend: the engine values each delivered kWh at `electricity_rate_usd_per_kwh`. | `LBNL or NREL fleet study of measured vs. modeled commercial PV output, ratio reported, <year, URL>`. |
| `upgrades.cool_roof.savings_mu` | decimal 0–1 | Roof area (building footprint) | Average fraction of the building's *whole* annual electricity spend saved when the entire roof is coated. The engine multiplies by size ÷ roof area. Cool roofs only cut cooling, so if a source says "15% of cooling energy" and cooling is 30% of use, enter 0.045. | `LBNL Heat Island Group or ORNL cool-roof calculator, Houston, low-slope roof, % cooling savings`, times `CBECS cooling end-use share for the building type`, showing the multiplication. |
| `upgrades.insulation.savings_mu` | decimal 0–1 | Wall area (envelope area) | Average fraction of the building's *whole* annual electricity spend saved when all exterior walls are insulated. The engine multiplies by size ÷ envelope area. | `DOE / PNNL Advanced Energy Retrofit Guide for Office Buildings, wall insulation measure, % whole-building savings, hot-humid climate, https://www.pnnl.gov/...`. |
| `upgrades.window_upgrade.savings_mu` | decimal 0–1 | Fixed | Average fraction of the building's *whole* annual electricity spend saved by replacing the south and west glass the feasibility rule sized. Not scaled, because that sized area is the whole target. | Same PNNL guide (window replacement measure, hot-humid climate) or an NREL/DOE window retrofit study, % whole-building savings, adjusted to south and west glass only with the adjustment shown. |
| `upgrades.heat_pump.savings_mu` | decimal 0–1 | Floor area (floor area served) | Average fraction of the building's *whole* annual electricity spend saved when the heat pump serves the whole floor area. The engine multiplies by size ÷ floor area. | `DOE / PNNL Advanced Energy Retrofit Guide, HVAC replacement measure, % whole-building savings`, or an HVAC end-use savings figure times the CBECS HVAC share. |
| `upgrades.led_retrofit.savings_mu` | decimal 0–1 | Floor area | Average fraction of the building's *whole* annual electricity spend saved when lighting on the whole floor is switched to LED. The engine multiplies by size ÷ floor area. If LED cuts lighting energy by 50% and lighting is 15% of use, enter 0.075, not 0.5. | `DOE SSL program, LED vs. fluorescent % lighting energy savings`, times `CBECS 2018 lighting end-use share, office`, showing the multiplication. |
| `upgrades.<type>.savings_sd` | decimal 0–1 | Same as that type's `savings_mu` | Uncertainty in `savings_mu` (1 standard deviation, same basis). Each scenario draws savings from a normal distribution with this spread, cut off at 0. | Range of measured savings across studies or field projects in the same source, e.g. `LBNL meta-analysis of N retrofit projects, interquartile range / 1.35`. |
| `upgrades.<type>.life_min` | years (integer) | — | Shortest useful life. Each scenario draws a whole-number life between `life_min` and `life_max`. | `ASHRAE equipment life expectancy table (median service life), <edition>`, or the manufacturer warranty plus a stated margin. |
| `upgrades.<type>.life_max` | years (integer) | — | Longest useful life. Must be ≥ `life_min`. | Same as above. |
| `upgrades.<type>.incentive_fraction` | decimal 0–1 | — | Share of `unit_cost` covered by a rebate, tax credit or deduction if the owner gets it. **Verify for all six types**: for each, name the program, confirm it is open to Houston commercial buildings now, and convert its rate to a fraction of cost. If no program applies, enter 0. For `rooftop_solar`, the 0.30 placeholder is the federal investment tax credit (IRC §48E) base rate; see `incentive_p` for its deadline. | `CenterPoint Energy Commercial Standard Offer Program, <measure>, $/kWh incentive, converted to fraction of cost, checked <date>, <URL>`. For federal programs, cite the IRS page or notice and the date checked. |
| `upgrades.<type>.incentive_p` | probability 0–1 | — | Chance the owner actually gets that incentive (program budget runs out, eligibility, paperwork, deadlines). Each scenario either gets it or doesn't. **`rooftop_solar`:** under the 2025 federal law (OBBBA), a solar project that begins construction after July 4, 2026 gets the §48E credit only if it is *placed in service by December 31, 2027*. Every project we recommend starts after that date, so `incentive_p` must be the chance the system is installed and running by Dec 31, 2027. It is not the 0.8 placeholder unless you can justify it. Include design, permitting, interconnection and equipment lead times, and lower it for larger systems. **Other types:** verify each program's own deadline and funding risk the same way. | Solar: `IRS Notice 2025-42 (beginning of construction for wind and solar facilities under §§45Y and 48E after OBBBA), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-25-42.pdf`, plus a source for typical commercial rooftop install timelines in Houston (installer quote or interconnection queue data, dated). Check also whether later Treasury guidance (e.g. Notice 2026-15) adds restrictions. |
| `upgrades.<type>.incentive_lag_years` | whole years ≥ 0 | — | How many years after install the owner receives the incentive. The engine discounts it by (1 + `discount_rate`)^lag. Use **1 for a tax credit or deduction** (claimed when the owner files taxes for the install year) and **0 for an upfront or point-of-sale rebate** paid at install. Placeholders: 1 for `rooftop_solar` (federal credit), 0 for the other five (assumed utility rebates). If the program you find for a type is a tax deduction such as §179D, change that type's lag to 1. | The same program page cited for `incentive_fraction`, showing how and when it pays, e.g. `CenterPoint Energy Commercial Standard Offer Program: incentive paid after measurement and verification, ~<N> months after install, checked <date>, <URL>`. |
| `upgrades.<type>.maint_frac_per_year` | decimal per year | — | Yearly upkeep cost as a fraction of `unit_cost`, at today's prices. Solar at 0.01 means 1% of install cost each year. The engine grows it each year at `global.general_inflation`. | `NREL ATB 2024, commercial PV fixed O&M $/kW-yr ÷ capex $/kW`, or a maintenance cost guide with the line item. |
| `upgrades.<type>.source` | text | — | Sources for every number in that upgrade's block. | List each, separated by `;`. |
